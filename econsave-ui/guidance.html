Principle 3

Help before the mistake happens

Instructions, live validation and a visible checkout trail so customers always know what's required and where they are — instead of guessing and hitting an error afterwards.

Try it: type a password to watch the requirement checklist update, choose a vendor category to see its helper text change, click "Simulate wrong password" to see the error wording, and step through the checkout trail.
