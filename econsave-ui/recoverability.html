Principle 4

Nothing is ever really lost

Mistakes are cheap to fix: a removed item can be brought back, a checkout step can be reversed without losing what was typed, and a forgotten password is reset, not a locked door.

Try it: remove an item and click Undo before the timer runs out. Then fill in the delivery address, move to Payment, and click Back — your address stays exactly as you left it.
