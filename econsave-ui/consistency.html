Principle 2

Same controls, every screen

Switch between the three screens below. The navigation bar, the wording, and the button styles never move or change — only the content underneath them does.

Try it: click through "Product list", "Product detail" and "My Orders". Notice the nav stays fixed, "Order" is used everywhere (never "Purchase" or "Transaction"), and Update / Cancel / Confirm always look and sit the same way.
